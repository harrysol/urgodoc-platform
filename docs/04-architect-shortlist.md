# 04 — Architect Shortlist (Miami / Miami Beach)

You legally need a **Florida-licensed architect** (and structural engineer) for a
signed/sealed permit set in the HVHZ. Below are well-regarded, currently-active
Miami / Miami Beach residential firms that handle renovations, additions, and
custom homes. **This is a starting shortlist to interview — not an endorsement.**
Verify license, insurance, and HVHZ/Miami Beach permitting experience yourself.

## Shortlist

| Firm | Why they're a fit | Notes |
|------|------------------|-------|
| **Strang Design** | Award-winning Miami modernist firm (est. 1998), strong residential portfolio | Higher-end / design-forward |
| **Choeff Levy Fischman** | "Tropical modern" custom homes — concrete + glass, indoor-outdoor; very Miami | Known for high-end residential |
| **Sebastian Eilert Architecture** | Full-service, sustainable; explicitly does **renovations & additions** | Good for green / efficient design |
| **MacKenzie Architects** | Renovation + addition work on Miami Beach residences | Renovation-focused |
| **Batroli Architecture** | Has done **additions to Miami Beach homes** specifically | Local addition experience |
| **MØ Builders & Co.** | Miami **design-build** firm: renovations, additions, custom | Single point of contact (design + build) |

> **Design-build vs. architect + separate GC:** A design-build firm (e.g., MØ)
> bundles design and construction under one contract — simpler, often faster, one
> throat to choke. A separate architect + bid-out general contractor gives you
> more design independence and competitive construction bids. For a structurally
> involved second-floor pop-top, many owners prefer design-build for coordination.

## How to vet them (do this for the final 3)

1. **License check** — verify the architect on the [Florida DBPR license portal](https://www.myfloridalicense.com/).
2. **HVHZ + Miami Beach permits** — ask for **2–3 recent second-story / vertical
   addition permits they've pulled in Miami Beach.** This is the key filter.
3. **Structural engineer** — do they have an in-house or trusted structural
   engineer for HVHZ? (You need one regardless.)
4. **Fee structure** — % of construction cost (typical 8–15% for residential),
   fixed fee, or hourly. Get it in writing, with phases.
5. **References** — call 2 past clients who did additions. Ask about budget
   adherence and how surprises were handled.
6. **Renderings/BIM** — confirm they deliver 3D renderings (most do now). You can
   bring your own AI concepts to speed alignment (see [docs/05](docs/05-ai-3d-rendering-guide.md)).

## Suggested process

- Send all 6 the same short brief (your goals + the property facts sheet + your AI
  concept images).
- Get **3 fee proposals**.
- Pick based on relevant addition experience + communication, not just price.
