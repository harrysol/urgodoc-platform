## What the photos confirm (drives the design)

- **Single-story, white stucco, tropical/MiMo-modern**, recently and cleanly remodeled.
- **Flat / low-slope roof** with thin dark fascia (small low-tile hip over the
  center entry). ✅ **A flat roof is the easiest, cheapest starting point to build *up*.**
- Long, **low horizontal massing** with a recessed central entry behind a screen
  of thin vertical columns; large **black-framed (impact) windows**; flanking wings.
- **Wide paver circular driveway**, white perimeter walls, lush mature landscaping.
- Interior: open living/dining, **French white oak floors**, modern kitchen,

**The setting:**

- **Unobstructed west-facing sunset views over the bay.**

**The street context:**

## Design implications (why a second floor makes sense here)

1. **Build up for the view.** The big upside at ground level is hidden by the
   single story. A second floor — primary suite + a **west-facing bay terrace** —
   turns "nice waterfront" into "elevated sunset views over the bay/ocean." This
   is the highest-value move and is reflected in the updated 3D massing
   ([../renders](../renders/)).
2. **Flat roof = favorable structure.** Building over an existing flat roof avoids
   demolishing a complex pitched roof; still requires the engineer to verify the
   CBS walls/foundation can carry the new load (see [03](03-feasibility-second-floor.md)).
3. **Keep the horizontal base, set the box back.** Preserve the low, wide
   street facade; place the second story over the central core, **stepped back
   from the street** and **opened up toward the water** at the rear.
4. **Match the existing palette** in any AI render: white stucco, black window
   frames, thin flat-roof fascia, wood/stone accents, vertical column screen.
